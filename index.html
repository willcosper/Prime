Shop rules this tool follows
  • Pieces cut together share one length, since the shear cuts the full coil width at once.
  • Six blades: a pass has at most 7 strips, counting repeated widths and rack-bonus strips.
  • Cut length is ordered length + 3" from 10' to 20'. Exactly 21' is cut at 21'. Nothing is cut past 21' for the allowance. Under 10' and over 21' are cut as ordered.
  • Leftover exactly 13" or 6 3/4" wide on cut lengths from 10' 3" to 21' is rack stock, not scrap.
